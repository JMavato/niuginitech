Our Business Profile

Who We Are

Niugini Tech is a Papua New Guinean-owned technology business founded in 2018. We focus on providing practical, reliable and affordable technology solutions for individuals, small businesses and organisations.

Our goal is to help customers stay connected, improve productivity and protect their premises through accessible IT support, networking, connectivity, CCTV security and digital services.

We take a customer-focused approach by understanding each requirement, recommending suitable solutions and providing clear communication and dependable technical support.

Helping Papua New Guinea stay connected through practical and accessible technology.

Founder & Leadership

Meet the Founder

Jeremy Markus

Founder | Technology Entrepreneur

Niugini Tech was founded in 2018 by Jeremy Markus, a self-proclaimed software engineer with a passion for technology, innovation and practical problem-solving.

His vision was to establish a Papua New Guinean technology business that makes IT services, connectivity and digital solutions more accessible and affordable to local individuals, businesses and organisations.

Through this vision, Niugini Tech continues to focus on delivering practical technology services, developing technical capabilities and helping customers make better use of technology.
